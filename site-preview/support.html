Questions people ask

Settings opens from the profile icon at the top right of Home.

1Using the app

Does MeFlex work offline?

Yes. After MeFlex has opened once with a signal, routines, the player, History, Saved, your own routines and Describe all work without one. If the web app is not opened for a while, your iPhone may remove its offline copy; it downloads again the next time it opens with a signal.

How do I put the web app on my Home Screen?

Open MeFlex in Safari, tap Share (the square with an arrow), then Add to Home Screen, then Add.

How do reminders work?

In the web app, open Settings, choose days and a time under Reminders, and tap “Add to my calendar”. That adds a repeating event to your phone’s calendar, and your calendar reminds you. To stop them, delete that event in your calendar app. In the iPhone app the button is “Set reminders”: reminders are notifications on your iPhone, iOS asks for permission first, and “Turn off reminders” cancels them.

2Your data

What does MeFlex keep about me?

Only what you put in, and only on your phone: your setup answers, history, own routines, Saved, routine times and settings. There is no account, no ads and no tracking. Nothing leaves your phone unless you make a backup file and send it somewhere yourself.

How do I back up my data?

Open Settings (the profile icon at the top right of Home) and choose “Back up my data”. MeFlex makes a file with your history, setup answers, your own routines, Saved, your settings, your reminder choice and your starting plan, and you choose where to keep it. To bring it back, choose “Restore from a backup”, pick the file, then “Replace with the backup”. Until you close MeFlex you can choose “Undo the restore”.

Why should I make backups?

Your data lives only on your device. iPhone can clear a website’s stored data after a long time without use, and deleting the app deletes its data. A backup is the only way to get your history back.

How do I start over?

Settings, Start over, “Reset app and show setup again”. This deletes your history, setup answers, your own routines, Saved, routine times, settings and reminder choice on that device, so make a backup first if you might want them later. Your analytics choice stays as it is. In the web app, a calendar event you already added stays in your calendar until you delete it there; in the iPhone app, reset also cancels reminders. “Restore defaults” is gentler: it only resets sound, colours, timing, default hold and weekly goal.

Does MeFlex send my data anywhere?

No accounts and no data selling. Optional anonymous usage statistics exist in the code but are switched off today. If they are switched on later, they start on by default, and you can turn them off in Settings → Privacy with “Share anonymous usage data”. The privacy policy lists what would be sent.

3How a stretch should feel

How should a stretch feel?

Health notice. Stretching should feel like mild discomfort, never pain. Move slowly, do not bounce, and stop if anything hurts. You never have to hold for the full time; shorten a hold, skip a stretch or end the routine whenever you like. MeFlex is a general fitness tool and is not medical advice. If you have a health condition or are unsure what is right for you, check with a qualified health professional first.

4Free, paying and cancelling

Is MeFlex free?

Yes, today. The web app has no account, no ads and nothing to buy. The first iPhone version is planned the same way, with no purchases.

Will there be a paid version?

Maybe later. The plan is MeFlex Plus, an optional App Store subscription. Nothing is charged today. Nothing will ever be charged unless you choose it in the App Store, which shows the price before you pay.

What would stay free?

In the current plan: today’s routine, a starter set of eight routines, three office routines and the youth routines, plus your own routines, the coach voice, the stretch library, History and backups. This plan may change before Plus launches; this page will say what is free when it does.

How would I cancel?

App Store subscriptions are handled by Apple, so you cancel in your iPhone’s Settings: tap your name, then Subscriptions, then MeFlex Plus, then Cancel Subscription. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews to avoid the next charge; if you cancel during the free trial, you are not charged.

Do I lose my history if I cancel?

No. History, your own routines and backups stay free and stay on your phone.
