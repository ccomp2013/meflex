Three directions for the MeFlex home page

The same words, the same app screens and the same live 30-second stretch, in three different art directions. Open each one, scroll it on a phone and on a computer, and pick the one that feels most like MeFlex.

  • A: Warm editorial

    A rink-side journal: large serif type, magazine pacing, light and dark pages.

  • B: Dark athletic

    Under the lights: close to the app, bold type, rink lines and strong motion.

  • C: Daylight calm

    First light: airy, quiet and precise, with soft morning light.
