                                    Settings

                                    Profile icon

                                    Shown on Home. Kept only on this device.

                                    Sound

                                    Release a slider to hear that sound. These are the same switches as the speaker button in the player.

                                    Colors

                                    Weekly goal

                                    Days a week you aim to stretch. History compares each week, and your 4-week consistency, with this goal.

                                    Reminders

                                    Days

                                    This adds a repeating event to your phone’s calendar. MeFlex can’t send notifications itself; your calendar reminds you. To stop the reminders, delete the event in your calendar app.

                                    Default hold

                                    Used for every stretch, on each side. A time you set inside a routine still wins.

                                    Timing

                                    Head start between stretches

                                    A short head start before each new stretch: its picture and time show while a small bubble counts down, then the timer starts. The 3-second switch between the two sides of a stretch always stays. The head start never counts toward your time.

                                    Your setup

                                    Stored only on this device. Never sent anywhere.

                                    Privacy

                                    Restores sound, colors, timing, default hold and weekly goal. Your routine times, history and privacy choice stay.

                                    Offline

                                    You're offline right now. Everything still works.

                                    After MeFlex has opened once with a signal, it opens and works without one: routines, the player, History, Saved, your own routines, ladders and Describe. If MeFlex isn't opened for a while, your iPhone may remove this offline copy; it downloads again the next time MeFlex opens with a signal.

                                    Your data

                                    History, your routines, Saved and your settings are kept only in this browser on this phone. A backup is a file you keep yourself; it never leaves your phone unless you share it.
