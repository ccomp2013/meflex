      How it works

      1. Build your list. In Add, search by store, brand or category and tap + Add. Or load the Black Friday list below.
      2. Set the order. In My list, tap ✨ Optimize for the shortest walk, then drag to adjust. 📌 pins a store in place.
      3. At the mall. Tap 📍 Locate. Follow the orange line. Tap I'm here to start a store's timer and Done to move on.
      4. If GPS is off (common indoors), tap Tap my spot and touch where you are. Routes restart from there.

      Settings

      Good to know

      • Map, store outlines, walkways, categories and regular hours come from Simon's own mall map (Mappedin), loaded live.
      • Mall hours Black Friday: 6 am–10 pm. “BF 6 am ✓” means Simon's store page confirms it. Otherwise the app shows the store's regular hours. Call if it matters.
      • GPS indoors drifts 10–40 m. The circle shows how sure it is.
      • Your list and progress stay on this phone. Share list sends a link with just the store list.
      • Add to Home Screen (Share → Add to Home Screen) to open it like an app. After one load it works on weak signal.
