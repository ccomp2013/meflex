      How it works

      • The map, store outlines and walkways come straight from Simon's own mall map (Mappedin). Walk times are measured along the real walkways.
      • 📍 uses your phone's GPS. Indoors it can drift 10–40 m, so the blue circle shows how sure it is. If it looks wrong, use Tap my spot and tap where you are.
      • When you're near your next store, a banner offers to start its timer. Done moves you on, Closed / later pushes it back, and Re-optimize reorders what's left from where you're standing.
      • Hours: “Opens 6 am ✓” means Simon's store page confirms it. rag & bone lists 12–7 pm regular hours, so it's last. Call (754) 200-7650. lululemon is still marked “coming soon”, so call (954) 908-8829.
      • Add to Home Screen (Share → Add to Home Screen) to open it like an app. After the first load it keeps working on weak signal.
